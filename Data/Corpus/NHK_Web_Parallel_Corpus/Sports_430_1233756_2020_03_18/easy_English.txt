In a meeting held on the 17th, the IOC decided to continue preparations to start the Tokyo Olympics on July 24th as scheduled. However, an IOC member who was a former member of Canada's ice hockey team wrote on Twitter, "The danger of the new coronavirus is greater than the Olympics. The IOC is being irresponsible." A athlete who won a gold medal in the pole vault at a previous Olympics also wrote on Twitter, "The IOC is endangering the health of athletes who must practice for the games." The president of the French Swimming Federation also said, "All pools are currently closed, so athletes cannot practice. The games must be held at a later date." As the new coronavirus spreads around the world, it is unclear when it will become safe. Because of this, opinions are emerging that it will be difficult to hold the games as scheduled.
